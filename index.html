rrealpath is inspired by the idea of peer-level engagement among business leaders in Southeast Asia. The goal is to coach an emerging generation of leaders in the region, empowering new levels of performance, knowledge, and cultural fluency.

Executive and entrepreneur coaching that supports a wide range of business challenges in Southeast Asia's cross-cultural work environments.

Most leaders reach a point where their experience does not fully address the challenge in front of them. The situation is less familiar, the reality is complex, and there is no readily available course of action.

A coach changes the outcome: not advice that's based on expert frameworks and best practices, but focused dialogue that engages and challenges a leader inside of his own work & life situation.

rrealpath works through native-speaker fluency in English, Thai and German, with experience across multinationals, SMEs and startups operating in Southeast Asia.

For leaders working between two cultures, or an organisation placing a coach with its people, the grounding is native to both East and West — not just translated from one language to the other.
