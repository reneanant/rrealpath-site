Privacy

This page explains what is collected, why, and how it is treated.

Getting in touch

The Connect form is the only place on this site where you enter personal information. When you send an enquiry, the name, email address, and message you provide are passed to Web3Forms, the service that delivers your message to coach@rrealpath.com. They are used only to read and reply to your enquiry, and are not sold, shared, or used for marketing.

Understanding how the site is used (Google Analytics)

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages and articles are read, how visitors arrive, and how the site performs. It reports on visits in aggregate — pages viewed, time on page, general location, and device — rather than identifying you personally. This helps decide what to write next and how to make the site better.

Session insights (Microsoft Clarity)

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors read the site. Clarity records interaction data — clicks, scrolling, mouse movement, and general usage — and presents it as aggregate heatmaps and individual session replays, which help us see which parts of an article hold attention. Form fields and any text you type are masked, and are never recorded.

By anonymised, we mean the data is not linked to your name or identity. Clarity does not receive your name, email address, or any account details, and the text on the pages you view is masked. Each visit is tagged with a random identifier so that a single session can be followed from start to finish, but that identifier is not tied to you as a person and is not used to recognise you on other sites or on later visits. What is recorded is limited to how the site is used, together with general technical details such as device type, browser, and approximate region (country or city). The data is processed by Microsoft on our behalf.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity each set their own cookies to measure usage and to recognise a browser across pages within a visit. These are used for analytics only. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will continue to work normally.

Questions or requests

If you have any questions about this page, or would like to know what information relates to you or ask for it to be removed, please write to coach@rrealpath.com.
