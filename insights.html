Insights Coach Connect

The big picture and the long view. Assembling knowledge, experience and capabilities into unique personal assets.

It’s not about what you have, it’s about what you do with it. Insights and ingenuity come from the intention to discover something unique and real that’s buried beneath the surface. rrealpath articles aim to do just this. We explore mainstream narratives and question the hidden assumptions to reveal a different kind of truth.

Basing the long view on the predictions of mainstream narratives is equivalent to moving with the herd. Nothing extraordinary ever comes from it. Building the big picture is about penetrating analysis, alternative perspectives and challenging the tidy consensus. It is about being wide open to contrarian points of view, and about trusting the discipline of your own analytical process.
